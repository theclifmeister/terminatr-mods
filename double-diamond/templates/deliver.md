# Deliver: {title}

Double diamond, Deliver: one piece of the picked option for a design, in
the order its `after` tasks set. Build it to production quality; the
design's problem statement, success criteria and answers are the spec.

## Steps

- Read the design, its answers and the picked spike's report
- Build it, with tests
- Check it against the design's success criteria
- Push and report, with a Check
