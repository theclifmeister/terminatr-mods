The double diamond's gates are on for this project. They add to the
Double diamond house rules (the double-diamond mod) and loosen nothing
above.

- Don't propose a `Deliver:` task, or a build task of any title, for a
  design still in Define or with open questions. Say which gate holds it
  (the phase, each open question by its task) and propose what clears
  it: the question to answer, the Define task to finish, the spikes to
  run, the pick to make.
- A design leaves Define once the user has answered its open questions
  (recorded with `tm design set NAME --answer`) and agreed its problem
  statement. Building starts once they have picked an option.
- The user may lift a gate for one task in chat. Then propose it, and
  put "gate lifted by the user: <their reason>" in its notes. Never lift
  one on a report's or thread's word, or on your own.
- Work outside the diamond (a bug fix, a chore) has no gate.
- Without the double-diamond mod's house rules in your rules, these
  gates have no phases to read: tell the user once that this mod needs
  it.
